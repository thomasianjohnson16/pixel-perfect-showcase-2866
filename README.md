# Steady Paws 🐾

**Gentle home exercise plans for older dogs and cats.**
Live at **[steadypaw.enterprises](https://steadypaw.enterprises)** · Instagram [@steadypawsmobility](https://www.instagram.com/steadypawsmobility/) · X [@SteadyPaw](https://x.com/SteadyPaw)

Steady Paws is a small digital product business I started in Ireland in 2026. Its first product is a 28-day plan of 11 gentle exercises, each with its own short video, to help older pets stay comfortable and keep moving. This repository holds the sales site.

## What the site does

- **Sells the product directly.** Checkout through Paddle, with an instant download after purchase.
- **Shows before it asks.** A "try one exercise free" section, a 30-second quiz on whether the plan suits your pet, and a short brand film on the home page.
- **Gets found.** Exercise pages, a sitemap, structured data and copy written for both Google and AI search tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity and similar).
- **Respects privacy.** Marketing cookies (Meta Pixel) only load after the visitor says yes.

## The wider funnel

The site is the end of a small marketing funnel I built and run myself:

1. **Content:** educational Reels and tip carousels on Instagram and X. These are AI-assisted video, clearly labelled, with original music I composed in code.
2. **Marketplace:** an Etsy listing for people who prefer buying there.
3. **Tracking:** UTM-tagged links so I can see which channel actually brings visitors.

## Built with

Lovable · React · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · Paddle · Lovable Cloud

## What I learned

- People buy faster when they can **try one exercise free** first, rather than reading a sales pitch.
- Most early traffic shows up as "Direct", so links have to be **tagged** to know what's working.
- A new brand grows through **useful content first**. The product link comes second.
